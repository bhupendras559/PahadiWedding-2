Almora aur Kumaon region un families ke liye meaningful ho sakta hai jo pahadi roots, family village connections aur traditional rituals ko celebration ka central part banana chahti hain. Exact venue aur access village-by-village vary karte hain.

Yahan wedding kyun plan karein?

Village wedding family connection, local food and traditions ko highlight kar sakti hai. Iske liye guest accommodation, sanitation, water, electricity, waste handling, parking aur emergency access ka realistic plan zaroori hai.

Pre-wedding shoot aur photography ideas

Traditional home courtyards (owner permission ke saath), terraced landscapes, village lanes aur mountain backdrops storytelling portraits ke liye ideas hain. Residents ki privacy ka respect karein aur private land par permission lein.

Ghoomne ke places aur guest itinerary

Almora town, local markets, cultural/temple sites aur nearby scenic viewpoints itinerary mein ho sakte hain. Specific attraction opening, access, road conditions aur group visit permission pehle verify karein; event schedule ko local travel time ke mutabik rakhein.

Season, travel aur weather

Weather, road conditions aur village infrastructure location ke hisaab se badalte hain. Monsoon disruption, winter cold, generator needs and local transport availability ko venue inspection mein assess karein.

Booking se pehle checklist

  • Village/venue owner and local permissions confirm karein
  • Guest toilets, water, power backup, waste and medical access plan karein
  • Catering capacity, local ingredients and serving logistics confirm karein
  • Guest pickup, parking, accommodation and emergency contact share karein
  • Photography privacy, sound timings, cleanup and cancellation terms agree karein
