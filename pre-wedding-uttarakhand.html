Frequently asked questions

Can you arrange a complete shoot and stay package?

Send your dates, group size and preferred area. We can help you explore options; availability and final package terms must be confirmed with each provider.

Do you guarantee photographer availability?

No. Availability depends on the date and location. We will not display a provider as available until it has been confirmed.

Can we plan a traditional Kumaoni-themed shoot?

Yes, you can request traditional attire and local styling. We will help you explore suitable local partners as they are onboarded.

Are drone shoots allowed everywhere?

No. Drone use and commercial photography may require permissions and can be restricted in some places. Confirm permissions before the shoot.
