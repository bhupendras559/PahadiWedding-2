Wedding services: what to plan and what to ask

Review the details below before opening the enquiry form. Each service depends on your location, wedding date, guest count and local availability.

Catering & Pahadi Food

Plan meals around your guest count, number of functions, dietary needs and serving schedule. Ask whether traditional Kumaoni dishes, cooking staff, crockery, drinking water and cleanup are included. पहाड़ी पकवान, menu, प्रति-meal खर्च और service timings पहले स्पष्ट करें।

Tent, Mandap & Decoration

Check the size of the venue, guest seating, stage, lighting, floral work, setup/teardown times and power needs. For mountain weather, ask about rain cover and wind-safe arrangements. मंडप, कुर्सियाँ, lighting, transport और weather backup quotation में लिखवाएँ।

Photography & Wedding Film

Discuss coverage for each ceremony, number of photographers, candid/traditional style, albums, edited videos, delivery timeline, travel and accommodation costs. Portfolio देखें और final deliverables तथा overtime charges लिखित में confirm करें।

Bridal, Groom & Family Makeup

Share the event schedule, getting-ready location, number of people, preferred looks and required services such as hair, draping or touch-ups. Confirm artist travel, products, start time and any trial charges. पहाड़ी गाँव तक artist के आने-जाने और early-morning timing की पुष्टि करें।

Guest Stay & Accommodation

Estimate rooms and nights, check distance from the venue, road access, hot water, parking, meal options and check-in arrangements. बुजुर्गों और बच्चों वाले मेहमानों के लिए venue से दूरी तथा transport की जरूरत भी देखें।

Transport & Local Coordination

List pickup points, arrival times, vehicle sizes, local trips and who will coordinate vendors on the wedding day. Hill roads, parking and late-night return trips may affect cost. स्टेशन pickup, गाँव तक सड़क और local contact पहले तय करें।

Before you enquire: quick planning checklist

  1. Location and date: district, village or nearest town, plus date or preferred month.
  2. Guest count and budget: estimated guests and a realistic overall budget range.
  3. Services needed: mark what you need and what your family will arrange independently.
  4. Compare quotations: check travel costs, advance, cancellation terms, inclusions and who owns each task.
  5. Confirm before paying: verify vendor availability, final amount and written terms first.

Once you have reviewed the service details and checklist, you can share all your requirements through one enquiry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I plan from Delhi or another city?

Yes. Share your village or destination, date, guest count, budget and services through the enquiry form. The details help start the conversation; they do not automatically confirm a booking.

Will every service come in one package?

That depends on the date and local vendor availability. Some services may need separate quotations. A package is confirmed only after its inclusions and terms are agreed in writing.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?

No. It starts the planning process. Confirm availability, final price, terms and payment directly with the service provider.
