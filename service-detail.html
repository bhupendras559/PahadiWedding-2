      Good to know

      Before you send an enquiry

      Do I need to know my exact wedding date?

      No. You can enquire even if the date or venue is not final. Mention that it is tentative in your message.

      Are prices and availability guaranteed?

      No. Rates and availability depend on the provider, date, location, guest count and final scope. Confirm a written quotation directly before paying.

      Does sending this form confirm a booking?

      No. It only sends an enquiry to PahadiWedding.in. No payment is collected through this form and no booking is confirmed.
