7 Practical Ways to Manage Your Wedding Budget

  1. Finalise the guest list early: Guest count affects food, seating, transport and accommodation costs.
  2. Consider nearby suppliers: Ask caterers, decorators and photographers about travel and setup fees before shortlisting.
  3. Separate essential and optional services: Prioritise the ceremony, food and guest comfort; decide on extra décor and add-ons later.
  4. Compare simple venues: Confirm permissions, facilities, water, power and parking at community halls, family properties or small local venues.
  5. Keep the menu practical: Plan around guest count and meal timings, and get per-person rates and serving terms in writing.
  6. Compare photography and décor packages: Confirm deliverables, coverage hours, transport, setup and teardown, and overtime charges.
  7. Plan for hill logistics: Budget for road access, older guests, rain or cold-weather backup, accommodation and supplier transport.
