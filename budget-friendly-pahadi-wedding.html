Har family ka budget alag hota hai. Hum fixed package ya guaranteed lowest price ka daawa nahi karte. Aapki date, location, guest count aur requirements ke mutabik available options aur vendor quotes compare karne mein madad ke liye enquiry bhejein.

Budget ko control mein rakhne ke 7 practical tareeke

  1. Guest list pehle final karein: Guest count food, seating, transport aur stay ka budget badalta hai.
  2. Local vendors ko preference dein: Venue ke paas ke caterers, decorators aur photographers se travel/setup charges pehle poochhein.
  3. Must-have aur optional services alag karein: Ceremony, food aur guest comfort ko priority dein; extra decor ya add-ons baad mein decide karein.
  4. Simple venue compare karein: Community hall, family property ya small local venue ki permission, facilities, water, power aur parking confirm karein.
  5. Menu ko practical rakhein: Guest count aur meal timings ke hisaab se menu banayein; per-person rate aur serving terms written mein lein.
  6. Photography aur decoration packages compare karein: Deliverables, hours, transport, setup/teardown aur overtime charges samjhein.
  7. Hill logistics ka plan banayein: Road access, elderly guests, rain/cold backup, accommodation aur vendor transport ko budget mein include karein.
