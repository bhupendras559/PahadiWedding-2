Uttarakhand Wedding Budget Planner

Pahadi shaadi ka budget banate waqt sirf venue aur khane ko nahi, balki guest travel, pahadi roads, weather backup aur local vendor logistics ko bhi plan karein.

Home / Wedding Guides / Budget Planner

Uttarakhand mein wedding ka kharcha guest count, season, venue, stay, travel aur aapke chosen services par depend karta hai. Isliye bina vendor quotes ke ek fixed total maan lena sahi nahi hai. Yeh guide aapko apna realistic budget banane, quotations compare karne aur last-minute surprises kam karne mein help karegi.

Planning rule: Pehle guest count aur total budget decide karein. Har vendor se written quotation lein aur confirm karein ki taxes, transport, setup, overtime aur cancellation terms included hain ya nahi.

1. Budget ko in categories mein baantein

  • Venue and accommodation: venue rent, guest rooms, check-in/check-out timing, electricity, cleaning, parking aur extra-hour charges.
  • Food and catering: per-person menu, children’s meals, drinking water, service staff, crockery, tea/snacks aur late-night meals.
  • Photography and video: event coverage hours, deliverables, albums, drone permissions where applicable, travel/stay and delivery timeline.
  • Decor and lighting: mandap, flowers, stage, seating, sound, power backup, installation/removal and weather-resistant setup.
  • Travel and logistics: guest pickup, hill-road travel time, parking, luggage movement, vendor transport and local coordination.
  • Clothing, makeup and rituals: outfits, jewellery, makeup, family requirements, puja materials and traditional arrangements.
  • Permissions and essentials: venue rules, sound restrictions, generator, first-aid, accessibility and any locally required approvals.

2. Pahadi destination wedding ke hidden costs

Mountain venues mein supplier travel, loading/unloading, narrow road access, limited parking, backup power aur rain/cold-weather arrangements extra cost la sakte hain. Venue dekhte waqt sirf photographs par rely na karein: road condition, vehicle access, mobile network, water supply, nearby medical help aur rooms ki actual capacity confirm karein.

3. Guest count se planning kaise karein

  1. Ek confirmed guest list aur ek tentative list banayein.
  2. Rooms aur meals ka estimate alag rakhein; har guest ko accommodation chahiye ya nahi, confirm karein.
  3. Har vendor ko same requirements bhejein, taaki quotations fair comparison ke liye equivalent hon.
  4. Advance, balance payment, cancellation/refund, overtime aur date-change policy written mein lein.
  5. Budget mein contingency amount alag rakhein; ise routine decoration upgrades par pehle se spend na karein.

4. Vendor se quote lete waqt poochhein

  • Final price mein kya-kya included aur excluded hai?
  • Travel, stay, transport, setup aur overtime extra hain?
  • Weather ya road disruption hone par backup plan kya hai?
  • Payment milestones aur cancellation terms kya hain?
  • Event date par team aur equipment availability written mein confirm hai?

5. Apni location ke hisaab se venue research karein

Ranikhet, Nainital, Almora aur Mukteshwar ke venue options compare karte waqt guest travel time, room inventory, season, weather backup aur vendor availability ko ek hi checklist par compare karein. Har venue ke current rates aur availability directly confirm karein; online photos ya indicative prices ko final quote na samjhein.

Printable budget checklist

  • Guest count and event schedule confirmed
  • Venue, rooms and food quotations received
  • Photography, decor and makeup scope agreed
  • Travel, parking and vendor logistics budgeted
  • Weather and power backup discussed
  • Payment, cancellation and refund terms saved
  • Final vendor contacts and event-day timeline shared with family
